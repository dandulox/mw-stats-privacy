MW Stats – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · v6.13 · Deutsche Fassung unten

This is a plain-language summary of our data processing, not a substitute for independent legal advice.

What data the extension processes

  • MakerWorld page data: while you browse makerworld.com, the extension reads the data the page already loads – for example model titles, tags, download, like and print counts, search results, feed contents and Creator Center figures.
  • Your MakerWorld account identifier: the user ID and handle of the logged-in account, used only to recognise your own models.
  • Your settings: language, sync interval and display options.

The extension does not read passwords, login tokens, payment data, messages or browsing activity outside makerworld.com.

Where the data is stored

All data is stored locally in your browser (Chrome extension storage and IndexedDB) by default. Since version 6, public model statistics can additionally be shared with Fomio's own server (see "Public data sharing" below) – everything else stays local, and this sharing is off unless you turn it on.

Public data sharing (optional, "Use & Share Public Data")

In the settings there is a single switch. When it is on, the extension periodically sends publicly visible model metrics – model ID, download/like/print/collection/comment/rating/boost/view counts and tags – to Fomio's API at api.fomio.de (encrypted via HTTPS), and in return you can see aggregated statistics contributed by other installations (e.g. community trend charts). When it is off ("Stay local"), nothing is sent and nothing is fetched – the extension behaves exactly as a purely local tool.

What is never sent as part of this switch: your MakerWorld login/session, account details, or Creator Center figures. (Creator Center figures are only ever sent if you additionally and separately enable "Points data sharing" below – and even then, only the specific points/downloads/prints totals described there, nothing else from the Creator Center.) On first use, the extension generates a random installation ID and a matching secret and stores both only in your browser; requests to the API are identified by these, not by your MakerWorld account, name, or email.

This installation ID does not by itself identify you. However, the model IDs shared alongside it correspond to models that are publicly visible on makerworld.com together with your creator handle – so a sufficiently motivated third party could look up a shared model ID on MakerWorld and connect it back to your public profile. We therefore treat this data as pseudonymous, not anonymous, and process it on the basis of your consent (see "Legal basis" below). It is not automatically deleted, but we will delete data tied to your installation on request (see "Your rights").

Points data sharing (optional, separate opt-in, "Points & Rewards")

This is a second, independent switch from public data sharing above, off by default. MakerWorld does not publish how many points a download/print/boost is worth; if you turn this on, the extension helps build a community-wide estimate. With it on, and only then, the extension reads:

  • your own points totals from MakerWorld's /points page (overall, regular, exclusive, boosts),
  • individual point transactions from the transactions tab of that page (which model, which day, how many points – also fetched periodically in the background so you don't have to page through it yourself), and
  • the lifetime points/downloads/prints summary of a model when you open its page in the Creator Center ("Model Data").

These, plus the IDs of your own models, are sent to api.fomio.de (HTTPS) the same way as the public model metrics above – identified only by the same installation ID, never by your MakerWorld account. The same pseudonymity note above applies. Never sent as part of this: your name, handle, or any message/account data beyond the points figures listed. With this switch off, the extension does not read these pages at all, not even locally.

Optional automation

If you turn the automation on, the extension opens pages of makerworld.com in a single background tab (for example your own uploads, the Creator Center or the home feeds), reads the data shown there and closes the tab again. It is switched off by default, you choose the steps and the schedule, and it never opens any other website.

Network requests

The extension makes requests to makerworld.com (to load your own published models and missing tags of models shown to you, using your existing session, like the website itself does) and, only if you enabled public data sharing and/or points data sharing, to api.fomio.de as described above.

No advertising, no data sales

MW Stats contains no advertising or third-party tracking code, and the extension itself runs no analytics of its own. Data is never sold or shared with third parties, and is used only to show you (and, for the data you choose to share, other users) statistics.

Legal basis for processing (Art. 6 GDPR)

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): public data sharing, points data sharing, and this website's analytics banner are all opt-in. You can withdraw consent at any time, with effect for the future, via the extension settings or the website's cookie/analytics banner.
  • Legitimate interest / pre-contractual measure (Art. 6(1)(b), (f) GDPR): replying to messages sent through the contact form.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectify (Art. 16), erase (Art. 17), restrict (Art. 18) or object to (Art. 21) the processing of your data, to data portability (Art. 20), and to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)) without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. To exercise any of these, use the contact form on mwstats.fomio.de or email info@fomio.de.

Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. As we are based in North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, the competent authority is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW), though you may also contact the authority in your own country or state of residence.

Hosting

api.fomio.de and mwstats.fomio.de run on a server operated by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestrasse 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany, physically located in Falkenstein, Germany. Data described on this page is processed and stored within Germany/the EU; we do not transfer it to third countries outside the EU/EEA.

Your control (extension)

In the extension settings you can export all data as JSON, delete market data, delete everything, or switch public data sharing on or off at any time. Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data. Previously shared public model statistics remain on the server by default (since, as explained above, they are pseudonymous rather than fully anonymous, and useful for other users' community trend comparisons) – contact us (see "Your rights") to have data tied to your installation deleted.

Contact

Questions or requests regarding your data: via the contact form on mwstats.fomio.de, by email at info@fomio.de, or via makerworld.com/@fomio.

This website (mwstats.fomio.de)

The above sections describe the browser extension. This website is a separate, static promotional page and processes data of its own, kept intentionally minimal:

  • Anonymous, cookie-less analytics – only after you consent: a banner asks for your consent before anything is sent. If you accept, each page load sends a page-view event to api.fomio.de: the page path, and, if you arrived via a link, the domain only of the referring site (e.g. "google.com", never the full URL, truncated in your browser before it is sent). Clicking an "install" button (Chrome Web Store, Edge Add-ons, Firefox) is recorded the same way, also only after consent. No cookie, IP address, or browser fingerprint is stored, and nothing is linked across visits or devices – each event is a standalone, genuinely anonymous data point (no ID of any kind is attached), used only to see how many people visit and which links they came from. These events are automatically deleted after 180 days. If you decline, only your decision itself is remembered locally (in your browser's local storage, not a cookie) so you are not asked again every visit; nothing else happens.
  • Contact form: if you use the contact form, the name, email address and message you enter are sent to api.fomio.de and stored so we can read and reply to your message. They are visible only to Fomio's admin panel (password + two-factor protected), are never sold or shared with third parties, and are kept until the request is resolved or you ask us to delete them (via the same contact channel).

Diese Website (mwstats.fomio.de)

Die obigen Abschnitte beschreiben die Browser-Erweiterung. Diese Website ist eine eigenständige, statische Werbeseite mit eigener, bewusst minimaler Datenverarbeitung:

  • Anonyme, cookie-lose Analyse – erst nach deiner Zustimmung: Ein Banner fragt vor jeder Übertragung um Zustimmung. Stimmst du zu, wird bei jedem Seitenaufruf ein Ereignis an api.fomio.de gesendet: der Seitenpfad und, falls du über einen Link gekommen bist, nur die Domain der verweisenden Seite (z. B. „google.com", nie die vollständige URL – schon in deinem Browser gekürzt, bevor sie gesendet wird). Ein Klick auf einen Installations-Button (Chrome Web Store, Edge Add-ons, Firefox) wird ebenso erfasst, ebenfalls nur nach Zustimmung. Es wird kein Cookie, keine IP-Adresse und kein Browser-Fingerabdruck gespeichert, und nichts wird über Besuche oder Geräte hinweg verknüpft – jedes Ereignis ist ein eigenständiger, tatsächlich anonymer Datenpunkt (keinerlei Kennung wird mitgeschickt), der nur zeigt, wie viele Personen die Seite besuchen und über welche Links. Diese Ereignisse werden automatisch nach 180 Tagen gelöscht. Lehnst du ab, wird nur diese Entscheidung selbst lokal gespeichert (im Local Storage deines Browsers, kein Cookie), damit du nicht bei jedem Besuch erneut gefragt wirst – sonst passiert nichts.
  • Kontaktformular: Nutzt du das Kontaktformular, werden Name, E-Mail-Adresse und Nachricht an api.fomio.de gesendet und gespeichert, damit wir antworten können. Sie sind nur im passwort- und 2FA-geschützten Admin-Bereich von Fomio sichtbar, werden nie verkauft oder an Dritte weitergegeben und bleiben gespeichert, bis die Anfrage erledigt ist oder du uns (über denselben Weg) um Löschung bittest.
